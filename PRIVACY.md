# Privacy policy

Clementine Remote controls the Clementine music player over your local network. It does not
collect, store or share any personal data, and it has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

- **Network.** The app only connects to the Clementine instance you choose, by address or
  through network discovery. Links in the About screen open in your browser.
- **Phone state.** If you allow it, the app notices incoming and outgoing calls so it can
  lower or pause Clementine's volume. Call information is used on the device and is never
  stored or sent anywhere.
- **Storage.** Songs you download from Clementine are saved to your device's Music folder.
- **Notifications.** Used to show playback controls and download progress.

Nothing leaves your device except the commands and requests sent to your own Clementine.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/clementine-player/Android-Remote/issues
