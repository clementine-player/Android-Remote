# Privacy policy

Clementine Remote controls the Clementine music player over your local network. It has no
analytics, advertising or tracking, and it doesn't send anything to us.

- **Local network.** The app only connects to the Clementine you choose, found on your local
  network or by its address.
- **Background updates.** While the phone is charging, the app connects to the last Clementine
  you used to update the music you keep on the phone. You can turn this off in Settings, with
  *Update kept music in the background*.
- **Storage.** The app keeps the address of your Clementine and its settings on your device.
  Songs you download from Clementine are saved in the download folder you choose, which is the
  app's own Music folder unless you change it.
- **Phone state.** If you allow it, the app notices incoming and outgoing calls so it can
  lower or pause Clementine's volume. Call information is used on the device and is never
  stored or sent anywhere.
- **Notifications** show playback controls and download progress.
- **Other apps.** Automation apps such as Tasker can use the app as a plugin, to control
  Clementine through it. They can only do that if you set them up to.
- **Links** in the About screen open in your browser.
- **Crash reports.** The app doesn't send crash reports itself. If you've let Android share
  usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play tells us when the app crashes, with the
  code it was running and the device's model and Android version, but nothing that identifies
  you.

Nothing else leaves your device except the commands and requests sent to your own Clementine.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/clementine-player/Android-Remote/issues
